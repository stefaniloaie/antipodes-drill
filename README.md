# Earth Drill Explorer

The Antipodes Earth Drill (Digging to the Other Side)

The Science: The radius of the Earth is 6,371 km. Over 90% of land points on Earth emerge directly in open ocean on the exact opposite side of the planet (the antipodal point).

The Visual Hook: A vertical cross-section drill visualizer. Users click anywhere on an interactive map to "drop a drill." The app animates a camera descending through the crust, mantle, outer core ($5,000^\circ\text{C}$), and inner core, popping out on a live 3D globe at the exact antipodal coordinate.

Viral Hook: Most people falsely assume digging straight down from Europe or North America lands them in China or Australia. Showing them emerging in the middle of the empty South Pacific Ocean creates instant curiosity.
